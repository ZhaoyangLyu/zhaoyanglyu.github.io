    About me

    I am a researcher with Shanghai AI Laboratory, working at the Physical Intelligence Center. I received my Ph.D. (2018-2022) from Multimedia Laboratory (MMLab) at CUHK, advised by Prof.Dahua Lin. I obtained my Bachelor's Degree (2014-2018) at Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research interests include 3D content generation and reconstruction, and embodied AI. I am looking for highly motivated interns in 3D generative models and embodied AI at Shanghai AI Laboratory. Drop me an email (lvzhaoyang@pjlab.org.cn) if you are interested.
